PRIVACY
Everything runs in your browser. Your designs are not uploaded anywhere. The only outside
connection the app can make is the optional Claude tab, and only if you add your own Claude
API key (it goes straight to api.anthropic.com from your browser, and is stored only in your
browser - it is never part of the app, your saved projects or your exports).
